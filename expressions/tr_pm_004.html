You're starting a video interview with a user, and they ask what they need to do.

질문

So what do you need from me today?

이렇게 말해보세요

평소처럼 써 보시면 된다고 말해보세요.

no right or wrong 정답은 없어요

just show me 그냥 보여 주세요

as you normally would 평소처럼

자연스러운 한 문장

There's no right or wrong. Just show me how you book a room, as you normally would.

정답은 없으니 평소처럼 회의실을 예약하는 과정을 보여 달라는 뜻이에요.
